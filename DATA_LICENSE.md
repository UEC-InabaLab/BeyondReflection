## Annotated KokoroChat

The files under `annotated_kokorochat/`, the derived data generated from them,
and the additional label files in `data/annotation_evaluation/` are based on:

> Zhiyang Qi, Takumasa Kaneko, Keiko Takamizo, Mariko Ukiyo, and Michimasa
> Inaba. 2025. *KokoroChat: A Japanese Psychological Counseling Dialogue
> Dataset Collected via Role-Playing by Trained Counselors.*

KokoroChat is distributed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivatives 4.0 International
license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/). The additional
counselor-strategy, distress-level, and disclosure-depth labels in this
repository are distributed under the same terms.

Original source: <https://github.com/UEC-InabaLab/KokoroChat>
